Who We Are

A Sanctuary Built for True Rest

My Nest Agro Farm Resort was born from a simple dream: to create a place where city dwellers could breathe deeply, slow down, and reconnect with the earth beneath their feet. Nestled in the verdant landscapes of Karnala, Panvel, our resort blends the authentic character of Maharashtra's farmland with curated luxury experiences.

Every corner of My Nest has been thoughtfully designed — from our organically managed farm and lush garden walkways to our pristine pool and lovingly prepared farm-to-table meals. We don't just offer accommodation; we offer transformation.

What We Stand For

Our Commitment to Your Experience

Four pillars that guide everything we do at My Nest.

🌾

Peaceful Farm Experience

Immerse yourself in the rhythm of farm life. Walk through organic vegetable gardens, meet friendly farm animals, and breathe the cleanest air Maharashtra has to offer. Our farm is more than a backdrop — it's a living, breathing part of your stay.

✨

Spotless Cleanliness

We take pride in maintaining impeccable hygiene standards across every room, bathroom, common area, and amenity. Our housekeeping team works tirelessly to ensure your space is always fresh, sanitised, and welcoming — every single day.

🤝

Genuine Friendly Service

At My Nest, you're not just a guest — you're family. Our warm, attentive staff are always present but never intrusive. Whether it's an extra pillow at midnight or a local sightseeing tip, we're here with a smile and genuine care.

💚

Unbeatable Value

Luxury doesn't have to mean unaffordable. We've worked hard to create an experience that punches well above its price — fresh food, premium amenities, beautiful surroundings — all at a price that leaves you feeling genuinely happy with every rupee spent.
