Frequently Asked Questions

Is there parking available?

Yes, we offer free secure parking for all guests on the resort premises.

Are pets allowed?

Absolutely! My Nest is a pet-friendly resort. Please inform us in advance so we can make arrangements.

Is food included in the stay?

Complimentary breakfast is included for all rooms. Lunch, dinner, and BBQ can be arranged at additional cost.

How far is the nearest station?

Panvel Railway Station is just a 4-minute walk away. Direct trains from CST and Thane are available.

What is the cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 48 hours before check-in. Contact us directly for special circumstances.

Can I visit for a day trip?

Yes! Day packages with pool access and meals are available. WhatsApp us to plan your day outing.
